# Support Policy

Routine support requests should be tagged with the relevant product area and answered from published documentation whenever possible.

Billing questions should include the account id, invoice date, and a plain-language summary of the customer's request.

Escalation is required when a support request includes legal risk, data deletion, suspected security incidents, or public-relations sensitivity. The escalation note should include the source message, the customer impact, and the requested deadline.

When the answer is not present in the approved documents, the assistant should say that the source material does not contain the answer and route the request to a human reviewer.
